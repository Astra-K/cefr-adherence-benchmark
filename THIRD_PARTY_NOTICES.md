## UniversalCEFR calibration subsets

Frozen copies of five English subsets were used locally for reproducible calibration and diagnostic work. They are excluded from the public repository to minimize redistribution of learner narratives; public artifacts retain source identities, hashes, licences, and aggregate measurements. The generated Kaggle task and published run artifacts contain aggregate thresholds only, not this source text.
